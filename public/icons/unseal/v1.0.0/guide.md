# Unseal textured icons

Approved Hexly presentation, composed without image generation. Native artwork: 2048 × 2048. Original foreground colors and position are preserved.

Use rounded WebP/PNG exports directly in site navigation and project cards, without an additional CSS mask, background or shadow. The continuous corners match the approved nine-project study; they approximate iOS styling and are not an official Apple mask.

Use icon-1024.png for iOS app delivery and apple-touch-icon.png for touch bookmarks; both are opaque square exports for the platform to mask. favicon.ico contains 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px PNG frames. icon.png and rounded.png retain the native canvas. Smaller exports are resampled from the native master.

Original identity and historical brand downloads retain their own provenance. Source-product adoption is a separate operation. Rights follow provenance.json and the referenced source/texture licenses.
