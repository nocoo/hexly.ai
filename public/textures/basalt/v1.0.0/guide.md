## Use

Use texture-light.webp or texture-dark.webp for full specimens. The PNGs are untouched native 1024 × 1024 responses; the WebP files are whole-canvas delivery encodings. Use the -320.webp files for cards. Light and dark were generated independently with the actual Workflow GPT Image Flare helper.

Keep aspect ratio 1:1, background-size: contain and background-repeat: no-repeat. Do not crop, stretch, mirror, patch edges or claim seamless repetition. Full specimens show the image at full opacity. Text-bearing regions use a separate background layer at opacity 0.37, softly masked at the left edge; text and Logo remain fully opaque. Use Hexly's real paper/ink tokens and minimum 4.5:1 normal text contrast. Measured worst-case contrasts at export are 4.940:1 light and 4.733:1 dark, including normal/hover surface colors before the mask.
