细长带状海草叶围出疏朗的近岸栖地，将中央留给海马。

## Scope

This v1.0.0 texture pack is independent of the existing Logo/brand kit. It decorates Hexly project pages and Hexly-created campaigns; it does not change the product's identity, palette, UI, release or archived status. Preserve official Logo bytes and colors: 99dd018d5be648af52f777a24eaac5dc627b8d17ff704f8ac2c5f1abc0fba3ad.

The approved identity is a seahorse. Coastal seagrass provides plausible shelter and holdfasts; species is not established, so no species-specific ecology is claimed. The background follows the animal habitat, not the database product's technical diagrams.

## Use

Use texture-light.webp or texture-dark.webp for full specimens. The PNGs are untouched native 1024 × 1024 responses; the WebP files are whole-canvas delivery encodings. Use the -320.webp files for cards. Light and dark were generated independently with the actual Workflow GPT Image Flare helper.

Keep aspect ratio 1:1, background-size: contain and background-repeat: no-repeat. Do not crop, stretch, mirror, patch edges or claim seamless repetition. Full specimens show the image at full opacity. Text-bearing regions use a separate background layer at opacity 0.37, softly masked at the left edge; text and Logo remain fully opaque. Use Hexly's real paper/ink tokens and minimum 4.5:1 normal text contrast. Measured worst-case contrasts at export are 4.991:1 light and 4.746:1 dark, including normal/hover surface colors before the mask.
