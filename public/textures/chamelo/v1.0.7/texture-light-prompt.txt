Decorative background surface for a project whose mark is a chameleon: botanical foliage from its real habitat — broad-leaved montane shrub foliage. One leafy branch with a few broad leaves, thin stems and small buds enters from the right edge and fills the right third of the sheet from top to bottom, like a pressed herbarium specimen photographed flat. The left two thirds stay plain neutral cream paper in the page's own tone. Low-chroma sage and grey-green foliage, soft even daylight, shallow believable relief, quiet and restrained. Orthographic flat material study, complete square canvas, no text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no user interface, no central emblem, no flowers, no tropical cliché, no yellow cast.
