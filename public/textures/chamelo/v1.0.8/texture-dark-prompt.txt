Tone-on-tone decorative background for a project whose mark is a chameleon: botanical foliage from its real habitat, broad-leaved montane shrub foliage. One leafy branch with broad leaves, thin stems and small buds enters from the right edge and fills the right third of the sheet from top to bottom, like a pressed herbarium specimen. Everything is extremely low contrast and near-monochrome: deep neutral green-slate paper of #27332c with the foliage reading only one or two steps lighter, a faint desaturated grey-green barely separated from the paper, like a watermark or blind emboss in shadow. Left two thirds plain paper. Soft flat even low light, no rim light. Orthographic flat material study, complete square canvas, no text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no user interface, no central emblem, no flowers, no bright green, no colour cast.
