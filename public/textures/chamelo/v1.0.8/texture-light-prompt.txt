Tone-on-tone decorative background for a project whose mark is a chameleon: botanical foliage from its real habitat, broad-leaved montane shrub foliage. One leafy branch with broad leaves, thin stems and small buds enters from the right edge and fills the right third of the sheet from top to bottom, like a pressed herbarium specimen. Everything is extremely low contrast and near-monochrome: pale neutral cream paper of #f2f2ea with the foliage reading only one or two steps darker, a faint warm-grey sage barely separated from the paper, like a watermark or blind emboss. Left two thirds plain paper. Soft flat even daylight, no shading drama. Orthographic flat material study, complete square canvas, no text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no user interface, no central emblem, no flowers, no saturated green, no yellow cast.
