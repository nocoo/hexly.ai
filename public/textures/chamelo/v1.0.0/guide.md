# Chamelo — Dunhuang plaster

Aged plaster with hairline cracks and restrained mineral pigment flecks.

斑驳砂壁，细裂纹与克制的矿物颜料斑点。

## Scope

This v1.0.0 texture pack is independent of the project's Logo and brand kit.
It decorates Hexly project pages and Hexly-created campaigns; it does not change
the product's identity, palette, UI, release or archived status. Preserve the
official Logo bytes and colors: 338f4041d0ce4c31c02163f403c051ed4f9ead04a2825eaf87b69fde9447d0aa.

The identity is painted in Dunhuang mural language and the approved finishing already uses weathered plaster light.

## Use

Use `texture-light.webp` or `texture-dark.webp` for full specimens. The PNGs are
untouched native 1024 × 1024 responses; the WebP files are whole-canvas delivery
encodings. Use the `-320.webp` files for cards.

Keep the 1:1 canvas complete: no crop, stretch, mirror or edge patching, and do not
claim seamless repetition. Text-bearing regions use a separate background layer at
opacity 0.5; text and Logo stay fully opaque. Keep ordinary text at
least 4.5:1 contrast in both themes.

The catalogue selects this pack through `Project.brandTexture`; the identity kit
remains authoritative for the Logo.
