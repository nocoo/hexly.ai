# Linie — Construction-grain field

Pressed drafting-guide furrows, fine unnumbered ruler notches and crossing construction impressions form a quiet engineered paper field.

压印的制图导引槽、无数字的细密刻线与交叉构造痕构成一张克制的工程纸面。

## Scope

This v1.0.0 texture pack is independent of the existing Logo/brand kit. It decorates Hexly project pages and Hexly-created campaigns; it does not change the product's identity, palette, UI, release or archived status. Preserve official Logo bytes and colors: c6c3b34616bf9ef4fa8969e56c275fe1122adc1dbefbe0a2baefcf09f01611a4.

Connect the template's actual coordinate rulers, drawing frames and measured tables to a credible pressed drawing surface. Use the approved full-square distribution, paper palette and shallow relief; preserve whole canvases. Banner presentation uses a separate left-edge opacity gradient, while text and Logo stay opaque.

## Use

Use texture-light.webp or texture-dark.webp for full specimens. The PNGs are untouched native 1024 × 1024 responses; the WebP files are whole-canvas delivery encodings. Use the -320.webp files for cards. Light and dark were generated independently with the actual Workflow GPT Image Flare helper.

Keep aspect ratio 1:1, background-size: contain and background-repeat: no-repeat. Do not crop, stretch, mirror, patch edges or claim seamless repetition. Full specimens show the image at full opacity. Text-bearing regions use a separate background layer at opacity 0.45, softly masked at the left edge; text and Logo remain fully opaque. Use Hexly's real paper/ink tokens and minimum 4.5:1 normal text contrast. Measured worst-case contrasts at export are 5.699:1 light and 5.683:1 dark, including normal/hover surface colors before the mask.
