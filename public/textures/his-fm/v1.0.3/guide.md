# his.fm — Stillwood paper

Pressed fir and fern fronds spread evenly across ivory handmade paper.

象牙手工纸上均匀铺开的冷杉与蕨类压痕。

## Scope

This v1.0.3 texture pack is independent of the project's Logo and brand kit.
It decorates Hexly project pages and Hexly-created campaigns; it does not change the
product's identity, palette, UI, release or archived status. Preserve the official
Logo bytes and colors: 0a64cac84ab3bbb8a1c5bcb03976b516698cb41cfec0ea2cdd9c5b5e1372cd85.

The RSS channel artwork is a stillwood; the surface carries the same foliage across the entire sheet so the card reads as one campaign material.

## Use

Use `texture-light.webp` or `texture-dark.webp` for full specimens. The PNGs are
untouched native 1024 × 1024 responses; the WebP files are whole-canvas delivery
encodings. Use the `-320.webp` files for cards.

Keep the 1:1 canvas complete: no crop, stretch, mirror or edge patching, and do not
claim seamless repetition. Text-bearing regions use a separate background layer at
opacity 0.3; text and Logo stay fully opaque. Measured worst-case body-text
contrast at this opacity is 4.849:1 (light) and 6.398:1 (dark).

The catalogue selects this pack through `Project.brandTexture`; the identity kit
remains authoritative for the Logo.

Composition: one restrained motif cluster sits in the lower-right quadrant; the upper-left and centre stay quiet so the card shows the same treatment as the rest of the collection.
