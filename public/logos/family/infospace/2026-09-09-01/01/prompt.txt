Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a refined physical-object project logo for InfoSpace, a native SwiftUI workspace SDK with resizable grids and customizable information panels.

SUBJECT AND MOMENT
Create one compact, shallow desktop information tray with adjustable cross-dividers and four unequal compartments. A deep-indigo anodized-metal outer tray has softly rounded physical corners and precise, slender rails. A small integrated brushed-nickel adjustment slider makes the movable divider legible. The divider has just settled slightly off center, making space for one larger item. Inside the bays, a few matte paper cards or very shallow card stacks use muted indigo, sea green, terracotta and lilac. The cards stay contained; the tray, divider and cards read as one close physical assembly. Broad shapes and clear occupied spaces take priority over tiny mechanisms. This represents arranging one's own information in adjustable panels.

STYLE AND CAMERA
Skeuomorphic, tactile and carefully made, with continuous physically credible materials. Restrained anodized-metal reflections, brushed fittings, subtly warm paper edges, precise seams and gentle bevels. Soft studio product light and sharp focus across the object. A gentle 75 mm product-camera analogy from about 40 degrees elevation reveals both the compartments and the tray's shallow depth. Let the model choose the exact camera distance, tilt and overlap. Keep the complete object large and compact within the square, roughly three quarters to four fifths of the canvas, with generous white margins around every corner. It should feel like a small useful instrument, not a shiny toy or a photorealistic room.

ISOLATED NATIVE FOREGROUND
Return one native 2048 by 2048 PNG on a perfectly uniform pure-white #FFFFFF matte. Every part of the object is opaque, including paper cards, metallic highlights and the floors of the tray compartments. Keep complete antialiased outer boundaries and physically distinct warm paper edges. Internal shading and material reflections belong to the object. No ground plane, horizon, projected cast shadow, background texture, external glow, haze, motion blur or translucent white fringe; those presentation layers will be composed separately. No pre-rounded app tile, inset border or medallion.

AVOID
No animals, mascot, low-poly drawing, faceted or fragmented surfaces, glass shards, detached confetti, glossy plastic, floating props, hands, planets, file-cabinet scene, computer screen, software screenshot, symbols, text, lettering, digits, branding or watermark. The tray and its adjustable spaces remain the entire identity.
