Create one production-quality physical-object logo for Coffee, an interactive coffee tasting and learning universe. The single finished mark is a beautiful ceramic cup of coffee resting on its matching saucer, with one small brass teaspoon tucked naturally along the saucer. Capture the quiet moment just before a first sip.

REFERENCE ORDER: Input 1 is Coffee's existing green coffee-bean sparkle icon. It supplies historical product context and a coffee-bean motif, not a composition to copy. The owner requested a new logo. Input 2 is an approved physical tally-counter illustration: use its convincing material craft, restrained product lighting and crisp isolated edges; do not copy its counter, button, numbers or blue paint. Input 3 is a presentation reference board for compact, slightly off-center framing and calm tactile hierarchy only. Do not copy its animals, backgrounds or motif paths. This project belongs to the physical material series, not the animal or faceted character series.

ART DIRECTION: One warm peach-pink stoneware cappuccino cup, a gently contrasting cream inner rim, a matching low saucer and a softly brushed antique-brass teaspoon. Rich cocoa-brown coffee and one simple creamy leaf of latte art are clearly visible from above. Broad continuous ceramic surfaces, subtle real glaze variation, a tangible lip and a rounded handle create a crafted object with believable volume. A small incised coffee-bean impression on the near wall can quietly recall the prior identity. The cup should feel elegant, substantial and welcoming. Use physically credible continuous shading, not triangles, polygon outlines, crystalline surfaces or plastic toy gloss. Keep ceramic highlights, milk foam, coffee and metal fully opaque.

CAMERA AND COMPOSITION: Treat the square as a product photographer's viewfinder at a gentle elevated three-quarter 70 mm perspective. The cup is the main visual mass, the complete saucer supports it, and the teaspoon is a small secondary gesture within the compact assembly. Give the image model freedom to find the most graceful angle and overlap. Let the rounded handle read clearly without widening the whole composition into a scene. Keep the complete cup, handle, saucer and spoon comfortably inside the canvas and away from its eventual rounded corners, with generous clean white borders. No cropped objects or distant decorations. Every surface and silhouette stays sharply rendered.

EXTRACTION CONTRACT: Render the object on an absolutely uniform pure white #FFFFFF background through every empty region, including the handle opening and all canvas edges. This is an isolated source for later foreground extraction. There is NO ground plane, NO cast shadow outside the objects, NO gray halo, NO vignette, NO external glow and NO colored backdrop. Self-shading and the real contact between cup, saucer and spoon belong to the physical objects. Do not add steam, smoke, haze or translucent fringes. Keep ivory highlights opaque and bounded by the actual ceramic. A related textured tile and soft presentation shadows will be composed separately after approval.

DELIVERY: One native 2048 by 2048 PNG, high quality. One complete cup-and-saucer identity only. No labels, text, wordmark, watermark, border, badge, collage, UI, mockup or additional coffee beans scattered around it. No upscaling.
