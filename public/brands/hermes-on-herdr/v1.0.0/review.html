Read the exact historical generation prompt
Create one production-quality physical-object logo for Hermes Gateway for Herdr, a terminal-pane gateway supervisor. Its job is to keep one dedicated agent gateway correctly bound, observable and controlled. Represent this with one compact crafted tabletop gateway, caught with its door gently opening while a single green status lens signals presence.

REFERENCE ORDER: Input 1 is an approved physical tally-counter illustration. Use its convincing compact construction, tactile enamel and brushed brass, broad restrained product lighting and sharp edges. Do not copy its counter shape, digits, plunger or ring. Input 2 is a presentation board: use its calm tactile hierarchy and compact, slightly off-center framing only. Do not copy its animals, backgrounds or pattern paths. This is the physical material series, with continuous credible shading, not a fragmented animal or polygon character.

THE OBJECT: One substantial upright gateway frame in softly brushed warm brass, with a thick petrol-blue enamel door held half-open by two small credible hinges. Its softened rectangular silhouette has a gently rounded top and rests on a very low structural foot that is part of the object. The broad dark-blue door fills most of the opening so that frame and door form a substantial, compact logo mass. A single tiny round green glass status lens is inset near the upper outer corner of the brass frame. On the door face, one simple ivory terminal prompt >_ is the only marking. The prompt is a small inlay, not a floating graphic or label. The door opening is the main gesture; the green lens is subordinate. Keep brass warm and satin, enamel deep and richly colored, and glass highlights small and contained. No padlock, key, guard, animal, wings, screen interface, network cables or unrelated props.

CAMERA AND LAYOUT: Imagine a gentle 75 mm product-camera view, a little above eye level and slightly from one side, with freedom to find a coherent door angle and attractive overlap. The full object sits subtly off-center in a square viewfinder. Preserve every frame corner, hinge, foot and door edge with generous clean borders and comfortable room for the eventual rounded-square outline. Do not place the object in an architectural scene, and do not crop any part. Avoid a thin empty arch with little visual mass. The main door and brass frame should stay recognizable at small icon sizes; microtexture is secondary.

EXTRACTION CONTRACT: Output a solid, fully opaque object on an absolutely uniform pure white #FFFFFF background, including the open space through the gateway and all canvas edges. No floor, no outside cast shadow, no gray halo, no backdrop, no enclosing tile, no vignette and no translucent outline. Physical self-shading between the door, hinge and frame is appropriate. The status lens has bright contained green material, with NO external glow, bloom, rays or reflected colored spill on the white matte. Metal highlights remain opaque and within the physical silhouette. The pale blue-gray paper field, unique routing motif and soft external shadows will be built separately after approval.

DELIVERY: One native 2048 by 2048 high-quality PNG. One single complete gateway object. No words or lettering beyond the small >_ terminal inlay. No watermark, collage, badge, logo sheet, UI or mockup. Keep all edges crisp and antialiased. Do not upscale.
