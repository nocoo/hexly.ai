Create ONE exceptional, memorable 3D skeuomorphic logo for ZEPPELIN, an original next-generation spacecraft fleet archive. This is a premium physical miniature spacecraft identity, with the confidence of an industrial design object and the elegance of a well-designed fleet insignia. No words, letters, numbers or surrounding badge.

REFERENCE ROLE: image 1 is an actual original HW ship prototype from the project. Learn its substantial naval hull, paired cylindrical propulsion pods, armored construction and command bridge. Do NOT reproduce its dense surface clutter, precise ship geometry, markings or dark scene. Design a distinct simplified symbolic command vessel, a compact and immediately legible identity for the fleet as a whole. The previous project's Z logo is rejected and is not a reference.

SUBJECT AND HIERARCHY: one compact broad ship, central armored prow projecting toward the lower left, paired substantial engine nacelles swept back toward the upper right, and a low bridge integrated into the spine. The silhouette should be bold, balanced, unmistakable and engineered, with a hint of a forward-moving chevron in the hull's shoulders. Central hull carries roughly two thirds of visual mass; the two nacelles are integrated close to the body, avoiding spindly wings or detached pieces. A single coherent group of muted acid-yellow enamel identification strips crosses the upper hull and the two nacelle collars. Two small warm amber recessed engine cores are material detail, not external glow. The layered prow, a smoked graphite bridge canopy, robust bevels and a few intentional seams give satisfying scale and tactility. Limit surface complexity: perhaps 12–18 broad designed panels, not hundreds of greebles. No visible weapons, turrets, flags, star fields, trails, hangar, pedestal, typography, numeral labels, ornamental Z, medallion or frame.

CAMERA AND COMPOSITION: three-quarter product-photography view about 35 degrees above, 75 mm equivalent perspective. Show a clear prow, generous dorsal surface and both propulsion masses with believable overlap. Let the model choose the exact pleasing angle. The ship fills a compact diagonal mass in the square; all tips, nacelles and corners remain intact with at least 12 percent comfortable whitespace around the whole silhouette. No part enters the outer margins. Keep everything sharp; no depth-of-field blur. It should feel like a finely crafted museum miniature captured in a decisive instant just before departure, rather than a game screenshot or a generic toy rocket.

MATERIAL: continuous physically credible 3D shading. Warm pale titanium and satin brushed aluminum armor, graphite joints and smoked obsidian canopy, carefully limited acid-yellow enamel strips, tiny warm amber thruster cores. Dominant pale titanium must be dark enough to retain a distinct fully opaque contour against white. Soft broad studio light from upper left creates real volume, controlled restrained metallic reflections and readable material separation. Fine machining, subtle ceramic surface texture, beautiful chamfers. No polygon facets, glass shards, rainbow details, chrome glare, childish plastic, cartoon outlines, heavy weathering or battle damage. A serious future fleet, refined and original.

EXTRACTION: render the one complete opaque spacecraft on perfectly uniform pure #FFFFFF. White spaces between nacelles and hull must be actual white where there is no physical part. Intrinsic occlusion between ship components belongs to the ship. Outside its silhouette: absolutely no cast shadow, floor, gradient, glow, haze, exhaust, ambient gray halo or transparency. Separate engineering-paper background and projected shadow will be added later. Pale metal highlights remain fully opaque. Return one native 2048 x 2048 high-quality PNG, no mockup board, no variations, no upscaling.
