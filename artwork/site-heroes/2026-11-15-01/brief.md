# Homepage hero artwork — four direction studies

The owner resumed the paused page artwork on 2026-11-15 ("之前暂停就是留给你现在做的"), asked for very high-quality, design-led header artwork with the homepage as the first example, and approved the four-direction plan: A Leaf Light, B Paper Diorama, C Herbarium Folio, D Orrery.

The artwork is a full-bleed stage for the right half of the homepage hero, generated directly on the page paper token so no extraction is needed. The six real, clickable family Logos stay as DOM elements layered on top; no family animal, bird or object is repainted. One terracotta focal point echoes the headline period. Rejected earlier languages (ceramic product shots, archive case, Three.js, flat screenprint) are not references.

Generation: one text-only request per direction, Azure OpenAI `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`, high quality, 2048 × 1536 (4:3, matching a ~720 × 544 slot at 2x), light theme only. Submitted sequentially with no automatic retry or model fallback. Every raw result stays pending for exact-byte owner review; dark variants, compositing and integration wait for approval.
