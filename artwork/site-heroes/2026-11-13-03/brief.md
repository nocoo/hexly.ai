## Direction

A flat, expressive editorial screenprint: a small abstract garden of ideas,
built from interlocking green and terracotta shapes. Clear silhouettes, broad
color areas, a little ink grain, generous negative space. The prior ceramic,
cream-product-photography, archive-case, instrument and toy-like directions are
not references. The approved site Logo is not redesigned or included.

Use the existing site's forest ink, moss, pale green, terracotta and paper field.
The model owns the exact botanical/geometric arrangement. This is a header
illustration, not a family Logo or a literal chart of project membership.
