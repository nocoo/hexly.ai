## Framing, color and exports

Protected features or the complete animal group have at least 214.5 px clearance from the actual 23% rounded outline. Deliberate neck/shoulder entries, where present, are recorded separately in framing evidence. [Framing evidence](verification/framing.json) records the actual outline measurement. [Matte evidence](verification/matte.json) verifies native opaque RGB. No stretched anatomy, cloned edge rows or hand-painted continuations are used.

Ten transparent, square and rounded PNG sizes cover 2048, 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px. The transparent, pure-white, square and rounded masters share one placement. The complete ears, mane tips, face and bird retain at least 214.5 px of protected clearance. Only natural lower/right shoulder entries cross the frame. Two native requests were made: study 01 was rejected because its bird stayed inside the mane; study 02 is selected.

The background is **Canter pleats**: base `#53817c`, light `#a4bdb0`, shade `#305651`, motif `#1e403c`. [palette.json](palette.json) stores exact native sRGB sample coordinates and separates artwork, presentation and existing product-theme evidence.
