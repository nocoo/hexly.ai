# Linie material identity

Measured in ink, edged in brass.

## Selection and history

Azure OpenAI `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`, one native 2048 × 2048 request with the accepted Dotty generation as a material-quality reference only. The owner reviewed the untouched result in Chrome and approved those exact bytes with “都通过，继续。”. No delegated acceptance is recorded.

Selected finishing `01`. The untouched [raw generation](raw/generated.png), [exact prompt](prompt.txt), ordered [reference roles](sources.json), sanitized request/response and [raw decision](raw-review.json) are preserved. Every numbered finishing directory is frozen, including its settings, masks, tool snapshot, independent background, shadows, exports and checksums.

The previous identity is the authored wireframe-cube favicon, preserved as [Linie's original backup](/logos/originals/linie-favicon.png) with the exact SVG source beside it at `public/logos/originals/linie-favicon.svg`.

## Material, placement and sizes

The whole set square has 188.03 px minimum clearance from the actual 23% rounded outline after uniform 88% placement, with zero clipped foreground pixels. [Framing evidence](verification/framing.json), [native-color preservation](verification/matte.json) and [all-size checks](verification/exports.json) record the measurements.

Transparent, square and rounded PNGs cover 2048, 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px; a pure-white master is preserved. At 128/64 px the triangular opening and brass edge remain clear. At 32/24 px the silhouette still reads as a set square; at 16 px the enamel outline and opening carry the mark, while machining grain and the brass reflection recede.

The background is **Set-square calibration field**, using base `#e7e8df`, light `#f5f6ee`, shade `#d2d8cf` and motif `#71877f`. Its exact geometry and opacity are archived in `presentation.json`. Project-specific projected floor shadows are separate; the transparent master contains no field, shadow or emission.

[palette.json](palette.json) separates native sRGB samples from designed presentation colors and the existing source tokens.
