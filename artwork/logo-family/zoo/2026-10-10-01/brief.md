Zoo is actually a static Chinese picture-learning website for children aged
1.5-3 with an adult. Its 246 cards cover eight categories; full-screen browsing,
pronunciation and device-local favorites are implemented. Preserve that factual
description rather than implying a developer utility from the chosen series.

## Physical analogy

A compact fan of thick, rounded picture cards represents selecting and turning
the site's learning cards. A printed friendly bear connects to the current bear
favicon without making the new identity a standalone animal mascot. Warm ivory
paperboard, a muted green card back and restrained ochre printing relate to the
actual source colors. These are art-direction proposals, not sampled new colors.
Use believable paper edges and gentle product photography, not glossy plastic,
connected animal facets, an app screenshot or an elaborate classroom scene.

Future light/dark supporting textures can use quiet pressed paper fibers and
sparse rounded-card registration impressions. They must be separate Flare
requests after the object direction is accepted, not a generic technical grid
or botanical habitat. Keep the source site's own theme unchanged.
