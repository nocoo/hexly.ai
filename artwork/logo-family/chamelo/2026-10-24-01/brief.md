# Chamelo chameleon head study

Chamelo is a considered Chinese-cultural colour atlas: original AI artwork becomes a
curated palette, a semantic website theme and a coding-agent brief. Its codename is
Chameleon and the first edition is Dunhuang Splendour.

## Owner direction

> onboard chamelo项目。动物系列，偏心布局的logo，变色龙，可爱不吓人，碎片化设计风格。

The owner authorized catalogue onboarding together with a new animal-family
identity. There is no liked original animal to preserve: the repository's only
existing mark is the designer `C` arc favicon, which stays as the previous
identity rather than a drawing reference. Product descriptions come from the
repository, never from the mascot's accessories.

## Direction

- Series: `animal`, drawn by the connected, irregular flat polygon language of
  the approved family drawing.
- Species: chameleon, unmistakable from the head.
- Composition: strongly off-center, head entering the viewfinder, generous
  negative space as counterweight.
- Tone: friendly and cute, never scary — no bared teeth, spikes, threat display
  or horror lighting.
- Multicolor and accents: left to the image model. No mandated prop.

## Request

- Model: `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst` via the Azure edits route.
- Source mode: `isolated-white`; one native 2048 × 2048 image on uniform white,
  no text, background tile, cast shadow or external glow.
- Exact-byte owner acceptance is required at the raw checkpoint before any
  extraction, finishing, catalogue preview or source adoption.
