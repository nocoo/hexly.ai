# Chamelo chameleon beast — Dunhuang mural direction

The owner withdrew the family drawing language for this project: no faceted
low-poly animal portrait, no glossy 3D cartoon. Keep the off-center composition,
take a Chinese cultural subject and paint a chameleon as ancient Chinese imagery
would imagine it, in Dunhuang mural style.

Studies `2026-10-24-01` and `2026-10-24-02` remain as rejected history; neither is
submitted as a reference.

## Subject

"变色龙" read through 古人意象: an auspicious beast (瑞兽), not a naturalistic
lizard. Long crested head, turret eyes, scaled serpentine body, clawed feet and a
curling spiral tail, with flowing silk ribbons or cloud scrolls leaving the
crest. Calm and friendly rather than fierce — it is a small charm, not a demon.

## Drawing language

Dunhuang mural: mineral pigments (石青 azurite, 石绿 malachite, 朱砂 cinnabar,
土红 ochre, gold leaf), flat decorative colour blocks inside fine dark ink
outlines, pigment granulation and weathered flecks, scrolling cloud/vine accents.
This replaces the family's flat polygon facets and every 3D/cartoon convention.

## Composition

Strongly off-center: the beast occupies one side of the square, its ribbons and
cloud scrolls trailing into generous empty space. The mark must survive at 64 px.
