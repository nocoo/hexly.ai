A chameleon as imagined in ancient Chinese imagery: a small auspicious beast for Chamelo, a Chinese-cultural colour atlas. Long crested head, turret-like eyes, scaled serpentine body, clawed feet and a curling spiral tail, with flowing silk ribbons leaving the crest. Dunhuang mural painting: mineral pigments — azurite blue, malachite green, cinnabar red, ochre and gold leaf — flat decorative colour blocks inside fine dark ink outlines, pigment granulation, weathered flecks and scrolling cloud accents. Calm and friendly, never fierce.

Strongly off-center composition: the beast occupies one side of the frame, its ribbons and cloud scrolls trailing into generous empty space. One native 2048 × 2048 painting on uniform white, without text, a background tile, frame or cast shadows.

Reference 1 guides only the mineral palette, mural material and silk-ribbon language; do not copy its landscape composition.
