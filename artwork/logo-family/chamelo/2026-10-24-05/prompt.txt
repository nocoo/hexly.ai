A chameleon head for Chamelo, painted as an ancient-imagined auspicious beast in Dunhuang mural style. Simple flat mineral-pigment shapes — azurite blue, malachite green, cinnabar red, ochre and a little gold leaf — with a few fine dark ink outlines and light pigment granulation. Long crested skull, turret-like eye, calm friendly expression, short neck. No jewelry, no earrings, no tassels, no beads, no clouds, no ornament scatter, no body.

Candid snapshot framing: the head enters diagonally from one corner, slightly tilted as if the animal had just climbed into the viewfinder by accident. About half of the square stays completely empty. At most one thin silk ribbon trails from the crest.

One native 2048 × 2048 painting on uniform white, without text, a background tile, border, frame or cast shadow.
