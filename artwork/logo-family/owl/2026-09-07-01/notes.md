## Framing, color and exports

Protected features or the complete animal group have at least 193.5 px clearance from the actual 23% rounded outline. Deliberate neck/shoulder entries, where present, are recorded separately in framing evidence. [Framing evidence](verification/framing.json) records the actual outline measurement. [Matte evidence](verification/matte.json) verifies native opaque RGB. No stretched anatomy, cloned edge rows or hand-painted continuations are used.

Ten transparent, square and rounded PNG sizes cover 2048, 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px. The transparent, pure-white, square and rounded masters share one placement. The complete bird and feather have at least 193.5 px of rounded-edge clearance. The native popover uses a transparent 22 pt mark; macOS app icons use a separate rounded tile with a platform inset. Browser specimens demonstrate the identity at small sizes.

The background is **Night aperture relief**: base `#5f6684`, light `#a8aec5`, shade `#363c55`, motif `#262a43`. [palette.json](palette.json) stores exact native sRGB sample coordinates and separates artwork, presentation and existing product-theme evidence.
