# Sleepy - material identity

## Owner direction

Onboard Sleepy and Rhino and generate their logos. Sleepy uses a skeuomorphic
tool identity; Rhino uses a fragmented, strongly off-center animal portrait.
Open the untouched generated images in Chrome for individual owner confirmation.
No raw-review waiver is granted.

## Proposed identity

One compact clothbound poetry book with softly fanned ivory pages and an
integrated brass crescent bookmark. A tangible reading tool rather than an
animal, sleep mask, pill or generic moon badge. Forest-green cloth and ivory
paper follow the source palette; the crescent recalls the prior identity.
