# Unseal — An opened green enamel and brass padlock

## Product

A macOS CLI that scans app quarantine flags and removes selected restrictions.

Source README at `ea82ce6bf69affc57e79a08a09a3e5aa648070bb`; historical catalogue descriptions are not the art brief.

## Direction

One exceptionally crafted opened padlock, its sturdy brass shackle lifting freely from a deep sage-green enamel body. Capture that small decisive instant just after release: the shackle is visibly open and slightly turned, while the compact main body remains calm and substantial. A small torn vermilion paper seal stays attached to the side as one restrained physical accent, not a flying ribbon. Real brushed brass, gently worn enamel edges and fine chamfers make a believable premium object. No key, letters, checkmark badge, hands, chain or extra props. A 75 mm three-quarter close product view around 25 degrees elevation makes the opening unmistakable, with the complete shackle, paper tab and body within the viewfinder.

## Separate presentation

Separated perforated seams and folded-tab outlines embossed in calm sage paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/unseal` page remain separate deliverables.
