# Bogo study 04: full-body framing rejected

The native response shows a complete standing buffalo, including all four legs. The owner clarified that the logo must remain a head close-up: natural quadruped anatomy should inform the visible neck base and shoulder without putting the torso or limbs in the picture.

The nose markings were removed and the flower sprig is the only multicolored accent group. This image is retained as anatomy and color context for [study 05](../2026-09-07-05/brief.md), not as a framing reference or approved candidate. No finishing or adoption was performed.
