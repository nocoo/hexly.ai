### Shoulders beyond the frame

The familiar wink and mouth-held flower sprig stay central. The shoulder continues through the lower-right edge, with the horns and ears comfortably inside the rounded boundary.

### Earth tones, a bright sprig

### Mineral warmth

Broken angular arches sit in a warm mineral field. Their shallow relief echoes the horns, while soft contact shadows separate the portrait from the paper surface.
