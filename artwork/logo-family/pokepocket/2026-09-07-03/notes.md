# Poké Pocket identity

A small pocket. A whole adventure.

## Source and acceptance

The owner-supplied JPEG is the native 960 × 960 source. Two earlier Azure requests were blocked without returning an image. This selected study performs transparent extraction and framing, with no generated portrait. The original JPEG, lossless decode, extraction tool, masks and source review remain archived. 1024/2048 exports are explicit upscales.

Selected finishing `01`. Every numbered finishing pass is frozen with the exact settings, tool snapshot, alpha, independent background, shadows, exports and checksums. The authorization and failed/superseded attempts remain in the archive.

## Framing and material

The complete cap, face, raised ball and fingers pass their recorded feature clearance. Only the lower jacket and forearm intentionally continue through the rounded viewfinder. Every delivered representation uses the same uniform placement. No stretched anatomy, circular bust cut, discarded physical highlight or invented source resolution is used.

[palette.json](palette.json) records actual native sRGB colors and coordinates separately from the designed **Pocket orbits** field and original product UI tokens. The field uses base `#af4b4a`, light `#c67668`, shade `#913e42` and motif `#682933`. Backgrounds, grain and cast shadows are separate from transparent app marks.

At 128/64 px, the red cap, face and raised red-white ball remain clear. At 32/24/16 px, the compact silhouette and dominant colors carry recognition; fine material detail, printed marks and background lines naturally merge. Native source: 960 px; 1024/2048 exports are resampling, not new detail.
