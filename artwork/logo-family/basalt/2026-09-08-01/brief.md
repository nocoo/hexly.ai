# Basalt — Hanbaiyu and candy-colored architecture

The owner likes the existing Forbidden City corner tower but finds its obsidian mass too dark. Preserve the architecture, tiered eaves, finial, low foundation and roughly 25-degree three-quarter camera. Change the foundation and balustrade to Chinese Hanbaiyu white marble, and redraw the tower with the iMac / iPhone 5C-inspired colors that already exist in Basalt.

## Evidence and visual hierarchy

[The repository palette](palette-reference.json) snapshots the exact accent and token files at the recorded local source revision. The selected light control colors are Blue, Pink, Green, Yellow and Pearl. The first four also match the chart's fixed candy colors; Pearl is the pale control swatch rather than chart gray. These are reference paint colors, not invented samples from the new artwork. Sample the returned native artwork separately after extraction.

Blue leads the roof mass with coherent green roof sections. Pink gives a small family of panels or columns warmth; yellow belongs in fine eave detail and the finial; pearl supplies light architectural structure. White marble carries the whole plinth and balustrade. Keep shadows physically plausible but open, with the broad black mass removed. Preserve high-quality material depth and the existing roof relief; the animal-series fragmentation rule does not apply.

The accepted prior raw is the primary edit target. The second reference is a deterministic board of exact source palette colors. The model may choose fine material transitions and surface assignment while retaining the tower's recognizable architecture. Keep every roof tip, finial and foundation corner intact. Final uniform placement must leave at least 128 px against the actual rounded outline at native 2048 px, with additional room where feasible.
