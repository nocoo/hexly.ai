## Material, placement and sizes

The whole object has 207.5 px minimum clearance from the actual 23% rounded outline, with zero clipped foreground pixels. All representations share one uniform placement. [Framing evidence](verification/framing.json), [native-color preservation](verification/matte.json), and [all-size checks](verification/exports.json) record the measurements. The unscaled extraction remains separate from the final placed foreground.

Transparent, square and rounded PNGs cover 2048, 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px; a pure-white master is also preserved. At 128/64 px the tiered roofs, obsidian base and emerald/amber accents establish the tower. At 32/24/16 px the transparent mark relies on the stepped architectural silhouette; tiny ruby inlays, railings and gold details naturally merge.

The background is **Corner tower construction grid**, using base `#ede6d8`, light `#faf7ee`, shade `#dbd1c0`, and motif `#a8987e`. Its actual geometry and opacity are archived in the recipe. Project-specific projected floor shadows are separate layers; the transparent master contains no presentation field, shadow or external emission.

[palette.json](palette.json) records native sRGB sample coordinates and source hashes separately from designed presentation colors and the existing website tokens. Product UI colors remain unchanged.
