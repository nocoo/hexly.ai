## Direction

One handsome compact reading folio: a forest-green textured leather cover holding a folded warm-ivory newspaper, with several layered paper corners visible and one small copper clip securing the upper edge. The slightly opening paper catches the decisive instant before reading. Use fine leather grain, dense opaque cream paper with softly visible fibers, crisp but natural folds, and a beautifully rounded brushed-copper clip. The newspaper should use tasteful abstract gray headline rules, column lines and one muted image block, not gibberish lettering, logos or legible article text. This is one coherent held-together object, not a full book scene. A 70 mm three-quarter view around 35 degrees elevation gives the folded pages and folio a rich compact silhouette. Keep every page corner and the complete clip within generous margins; no flying sheets, hand, cup or reading glasses.

## Separate presentation

Staggered editorial columns, fine baseline rules and an offset paper-fold seam in warm olive paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/geekhub` page remain separate deliverables.
