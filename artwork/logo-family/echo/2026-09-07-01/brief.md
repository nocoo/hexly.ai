## Direction

One exquisite pocket surveying compass, a chunky circular brushed-brass case with a midnight-navy dial, a clear but optically restrained glass face, and a single vivid red-and-ivory compass needle caught settling toward north. Fine bearing ticks and a quietly embossed compass rose give truthful instrument detail without readable labels. A small folded hanging ring and the slightly raised hinge sit close to the substantial case, never forming a distant accessory. Keep it a tangible precision tool with believable metal thickness and gently softened edges, not a flat app glyph or floating map pin. Use a 75 mm three-quarter product perspective about 35 degrees above, allowing the image to choose the most legible dial angle. No map, pedestal, chain, glow outside the object or extra props.

## Separate presentation

Latitude arcs, a sparse meridian fan and short bearing marks in pale harbor-blue paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/echo` page remain separate deliverables.
