# Octo: return to the approved animal-family language

The owner rejected study 03 and asked to revisit previous animal-family logos: fragmented, cute, strongly off-center, predominantly blue, with colorful highlights.

Inspected approved Pew, Falcon and Kite raw images and their owner review records. Their important features are expressive head close-ups, deliberate empty space, connected facets of varied scale, and local color highlights within a dominant palette. Study 03 lacked facial character and multicolored accents and used a loose whole-body composition.

Submit Falcon and Pew only as drawing-language references. Do not use Hooky, Frogie or any rejected Octo image. No reference anatomy or species is transferred. The new identity remains a jellyfish at the owner's explicit direction, despite the Octo name.

New exact-byte approval is required. Preserve raw output without processing; report decoded native format and size, not the requested resolution. Other five project approvals remain unchanged.
