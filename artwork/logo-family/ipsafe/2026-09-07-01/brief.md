# IPSafe — A green network cable tester with a short patch lead

## Product

A CLI that checks a network condition before allowing a command to execute.

Source README at `aab8f669fa8cb1b955d6ce7d9431e42c09509428`; historical catalogue descriptions are not the art brief.

## Direction

One compact professional Ethernet cable tester with a deep-green rubber bumper and a brushed charcoal-metal face. A short cream patch cable loops neatly from the top back to a neighboring port, with believable translucent smoky RJ45 connector housings. Keep that cable loop close and broad enough to read as one small attached gesture, while the sturdy rectangular instrument remains primary. Three small green indicator lamps are solid luminous surfaces, showing a good connection; no exterior glow and no readable labels or numbers. Capture the calm instant when the test has passed. Fine metal machining, real molded rubber seams and cable texture should make it a high-quality tangible object. A 70 mm product perspective roughly 30 degrees elevated should show the face, top ports and complete compact cable loop. No padlock, shield glyph, monitor, hand, long trailing wire or extra props.

## Separate presentation

Paired port rectangles, guarded chevron lanes and three short status bars in quiet mint paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/ipsafe` page remain separate deliverables.
