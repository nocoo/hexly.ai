## Object and decisive moment

One compact resettable circuit-breaker module: a substantial graphite housing,
warm ivory face insert, a single terracotta-red rocker lever at its mechanical
detent, a satin steel pivot collar and two short warm brass terminals. The
lever is the only focal control. Stopping at a physical limit suggests policy
boundaries; the resettable mechanism suggests a deliberately finite second
attempt. Use believable continuous 3D materials and real thickness, not polygon
facets, a mascot, toy, shield, lock badge or abstract SaaS glyph.

A 75 mm three-quarter product view, about 30 degrees above the face, shows the
rocker, side thickness and short terminals together. The whole compact assembly
fills roughly three quarters of the native square, with every corner and
projection clear of a later rounded outline. The object remains complete:
no post-generation crop, detached parts, long wires, hand or secondary prop.
