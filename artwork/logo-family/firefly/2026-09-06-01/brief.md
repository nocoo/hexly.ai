# Firefly 01 — The quiet lantern

## Purpose

Firefly is a writing and publishing platform, currently used for `lizheng.blog`. The owner describes its role as helping people find their way through ideas. The existing prismatic flame is explicitly disliked and is a historical comparison, not a composition to preserve.

## Concept

A complete, gently hovering firefly is its own little lantern. A warm amber abdomen is the primary recognition cue; paired antennae, beetle wing cases, and a clear insect silhouette distinguish it from a generic flame. Its partly opened wing cases have the rhythm of a book opening, without adding a literal book or a handheld prop. The upward diagonal suggests curiosity and forward movement.

Deep blue-green anatomy gives the warm light a clear counterweight. A few connected turquoise, blue, coral, and violet inlays retain the family's controlled multicolored accents. Large readable masses take priority over small facets.

## Explicit art-direction exception

For Firefly, the owner authorized a complete redesign and a more skeuomorphic rendering while remaining in the same family. Use gently sculpted matte surfaces, restrained satin highlights, and an internally luminous amber abdomen. Preserve joined color planes and selected faceted inlays, but do not force every curved surface into a flat triangle. This is a documented exception for this study, not a change to the approved Frogie or Pew artwork.

The family connection comes from the animal character, offset composition, dominant hue with controlled colorful accents, related tonal background, quiet curved motifs, and soft shallow shadows. No pixel grid, photorealistic insect hairs, glass-shard flame, detached confetti, or busy scene.

Archive the untouched output, exact prompt, sanitized request/response, reference hashes, and each finishing pass. Deliver transparent, square, rounded, and pure-white masters, all ten required PNG sizes, a complete static review, and `/logos/firefly` with matching before/after and size checks. Sample the artwork palette after generation; presentation colors are proposed and stay separate from the existing site primary `#3C83F6`.
