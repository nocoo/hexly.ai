## Direction and disposition

The owner requested a tactile campfire with small surrounding fireflies and natural material colors. This output shows a tall angular flame, crossed split logs, coals, an earthy ground, and four small insects on a dark background. The flame has a pronounced crystalline shape; the logs and ground are materially detailed. These are observations, not an owner decision.

The untouched image was displayed immediately and opened in Chrome. Before selecting it, the owner supplied a new Downloads reference showing a single flying firefly in front of a flame, and requested its action recomposed in the family style with a clean night background, less pixelation, and a larger subject. Study 06 follows that reference. The campfire scene is superseded and unselected; the owner did not explicitly approve or aesthetically reject these bytes. Its raw review therefore remains pending with a superseded disposition.
