## Scene and composition

A close three-quarter view of one compact campfire on a quiet dark earthen surface. Three substantial split logs cross at the lower center, with a few small coals and a low ember bed. A broad, joined amber flame rises above them, slightly left of center. Four small fireflies hover at unequal distances around the upper and side spaces, tracing an implied loose arc through their placements rather than a drawn orbit.

The fire, wood, coals, ground reflections, and small insects form a balanced square composition. Fullness comes from these overlapping objects and their light relationships. The log base supplies width; the flame supplies height; the fireflies balance the surrounding spaces. Keep all recognizable subjects inside a safe border, with no circular plinth, badge, wide landscape, forest panorama, campsite props, or horizon.

Each firefly is a small neutral bronze/olive body with discreet wings and a pale yellow-green tail light. No human expression, oversized eyes, exposed hooked legs, mitten hands, smiling antenna character, or enlarged macro anatomy. Their small scale provides calmness. Variation in angle, depth, and tail-light intensity adds natural life without visual clutter.

## Material, light, and fragmentation

Use a tactile, deliberately modeled three-dimensional icon scene. Wood has warm brown cut ends, restrained grain, charred outer planes, and believable contact shadows. Coals carry dim red/orange emission. Fire has a warm ivory/gold core, amber/orange edges, and restrained bloom. Firefly light is pale yellow-green, distinct from the warmer fire. Natural reflected light supplies color variety; do not add rainbow bands to wood, flames, or insects.

Retain family structure through selective broad facets: visibly joined angular flame lobes, split-log planes, carved wood ends, and a few small wing planes. Use irregular connected surfaces rather than uniform low-poly triangulation. The flame must still read as luminous fire, not an opaque crystal, candle, or plastic sculpture. Soft emitted light, occlusion, and matte surfaces establish volume. Fragmentation supports a coherent scene instead of becoming an all-over texture.

The background is deep ink-green/charcoal with subtle cool ambient light and fine grain. A warm light pool anchors the campfire to the ground; faint rising heat or smoke can provide project-specific background rhythm. No borrowed Frogie curves or Pew stripes. The old sea-glass light-orbit plans remain unselected in previous studies and are not carried into this scene.
