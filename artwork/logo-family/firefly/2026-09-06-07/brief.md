# Firefly 07 — A clean foreground for separate light

## Owner direction

The owner called study 06 an improvement and requested a new model output with an easily removable background. The nighttime presentation should be composited afterward, and emitted glow should also be handled independently because it complicates extraction.

Use the exact study 06 image as the sole edit reference. Retain its hovering gesture, composition, scale, connected facets, bronze body, champagne wings, cool edge accents, separate flame, and luminous-looking abdomen. This is a targeted separation edit, not a new mascot or pose exploration. The full integrated scene remains archived in study 06; its positive directional feedback does not approve any future generated bytes.

## Source requirements

Request one native 2048 × 2048 opaque PNG. Every unoccupied pixel is uniform white `#FFFFFF`, including gaps between antennae, wings, body, and flame. Remove the crescent, stars, clouds, night field, outside shadows, light spill, haze, and external bloom. Preserve complete, antialiased foreground boundaries and all detached flame accents.

The flame and abdomen remain solid opaque colored artwork with visible gold and orange facets. Pale highlights belong inside their hard boundaries. Do not generate translucent wings, soft glowing fringes, white halos, or ambient color on the backdrop. White removal must be able to separate the foreground without destroying intentional light effects, because those effects are not part of this source.

## Planned layers after confirmation

1. Independently drawn dark blue-green night background with restrained crescent, star, and cloud relief.
2. Independently adjustable warm background light from the flame and abdomen, with separate masks and settings.
3. Any necessary shallow presentation shadow, kept separate from the artwork.
4. The extracted foreground with its opaque source colors preserved.
5. Optional restrained near-source light overlay, stored separately from the clean transparent master.

The transparent master remains free of glow and background. Square and rounded presentations may include the selected light layers. Preserve masks, layer images, blend settings, colors, positions, radii, opacities, and hashes in the finishing archive. Plan details live in `presentation-plan.json`; no layers have been rendered yet.
