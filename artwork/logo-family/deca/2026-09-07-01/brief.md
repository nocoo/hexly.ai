# Deca — A midnight teal rotary desk telephone

## Product

A local-first macOS AI gateway connecting Discord, the terminal and HTTP.

Source README at `682e948fb9f8bb3f21a43988304435ba1c991654`; historical catalogue descriptions are not the art brief.

## Direction

One beautifully restored rotary desk telephone, in deep midnight-teal Bakelite with a warm ivory rotary dial and discreet brass hardware. The receiver rests slightly diagonally on its cradle, as if a conversation has just ended, while a short coiled cord curls closely beside the body as one coherent gesture. Capture that familiar tactile decisive instant; no hand or separate handset floating far away. Give the Bakelite rich fine surface grain, soft broad reflections, gentle age at contact edges and convincing physical weight. Keep the phone compact and broad, with clear receiver ends and a substantial curved base. A 70 mm product perspective about 30 degrees elevated sees the dial and the whole receiver. No lettering, digital screen, plug cut off by the frame, extra speech bubbles or background furniture.

## Separate presentation

Three rounded switchboard routing paths with discrete paired terminals in cool blue-gray paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/deca` page remain separate deliverables.
