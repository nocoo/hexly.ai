Read the exact generation prompt
Create one exquisite square Logo for DIORAMA JOURNEY: an agent tool that explains a story by carrying one recurring object through connected miniature architectural worlds. This is a skeuomorphic 3D physical-object identity, a small crafted architectural maquette. Output the object only, isolated on perfectly uniform white, with no lettering.

REFERENCE PRIORITY
Image 1 is the actual project's original 3D story example. Take its cream arched gateway, restrained terracotta cap, sage and mint model trees, warm stone pathway, substantial little island base, and especially the single amber lantern that travels through the story. Keep this recognizable world and its gentle material colors. It is a scene reference, not a layout to reproduce: omit its text, decorative graphic rings, editorial composition, border and the long bridge that leaves the frame.
Image 2 is a family presentation reference. It guides calm tactile finish, broad soft illumination and charmingly deliberate composition only. Do not copy an animal, character face, surrounding colored tile or palette from that board. The requested subject is an original physical miniature derived from image 1.

THE OBJECT AND ITS MOMENT
Imagine a beautifully made palm-sized architectural story model, photographed at the instant a little amber lantern arrives at a new chapter. One low, substantial, softly bevelled octagonal island carries a warm cream arched gateway with a modest terracotta lintel cap. A few broad ivory stone pavers form a short gently curving journey toward and through the gateway. The visible front and side of the island have believable layered cream and warm limestone construction; its top is muted sage green. One compact conical miniature tree and one softer rounded miniature tree flank the architecture. Their stems and canopies stay integral to this one compact assembly.

The ONE lantern sits near the front of the pathway, just before the threshold: a rounded opaque amber globe with a small brass foot, cap and complete carrying loop, inspired by the lantern in image 1. Make it a clear, warm secondary focal point, about one fifth of the arch's height, large enough to recognize but subordinate to the architecture and island. Its construction is simple and plausible. The warm color comes from within its opaque material; no external light rays or bloom. No second lantern, human, animal, mascot face, floating confetti, oversized flower or detached decoration. The gateway, path and lantern together tell the story without symbols or text. Favor a few beautiful substantial parts over many tiny details.

COMPOSITION AND CAMERA
Treat the square as a product photographer's viewfinder capturing this miniature's decisive moment. Use a gentle approximately 70 mm perspective, viewed from roughly 35–40 degrees above so that the path, arch opening and depth of the base are all visible. Let the exact camera angle, placement and overlap form a convincing, engaging object. The complete assembly should feel visually full and compact, with the broad base and gateway carrying its mass. Keep the entire island, cap corners, trees, lantern handle and every other part comfortably inside the square, with generous white space around the complete silhouette for a future rounded-square presentation. Nothing touches or crosses the image edges. Avoid a wide landscape or distant islands, a tall thin empty structure, extreme perspective and an enormous vacant arch opening. The object is the Logo; there is no surrounding badge, tile, circle or frame.

MATERIAL AND COLOR
Beautiful matte ceramic and painted architectural model wood, softly bevelled warm limestone, a little restrained brushed brass. Continuous physically credible 3D form, refined smooth shading, broad soft upper-left light and quiet material texture. Keep sculptural silhouettes clear and focused throughout; no depth-of-field blur. Sage and celadon greens form the dominant calm family, balanced by warm ivory architecture and a restrained terracotta cap. Use the project's actual scene palette as direction: green #668b70, mint #a8c7a9, moss #4f7455, cream #fff3d8, warm stone #eed7b1, terracotta #ce775b, amber gold #e7b552 and brass #bd8940. Preserve natural separation between these materials. The lantern supplies the most luminous warm accent. No neon, black outlines, polygon shards, animal-family facets, glossy toy plastic or glassy translucent surfaces.

EXTRACTION SOURCE, NOT THE FINAL PRESENTATION
Deliver the complete opaque miniature on a perfectly uniform pure #FFFFFF matte. Cream stone and pale green details must have fully opaque boundaries and remain visibly distinct from the white background. The open space through the arch and through the lantern handle is clean white wherever no actual object is behind it. Intrinsic shading and contact shadows between physical parts belong to the object. Outside its silhouette there must be NO floor, horizon, cast shadow, gray halo, ambient gradient, glow, fog or translucent fringe. The future sage presentation background, paper texture and projected shadow are separate layers and must not be baked into this source. No typography, initials, numbers, watermarks, mockup sheet or multiple variants.

Return ONE native high-quality 2048 × 2048 PNG with crisp antialiased boundaries, not an upscale. A collectible miniature architectural object that feels unmistakably like the supplied project's world and remains a coherent Logo when small.
