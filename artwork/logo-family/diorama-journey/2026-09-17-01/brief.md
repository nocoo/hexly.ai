## Art direction

- Catalogue category: `skills`; visual series: `material` (skeuomorphic 3D tool).
- Subject: one complete architectural maquette on a low eight-sided base,
  carrying a cream arch with a terracotta cap, a sage stone path, two compact
  model trees and one small amber lantern approaching the threshold.
- Moment: the lantern has reached the next chapter. The path and portal convey
  connected storytelling while the complete physical miniature stays readable
  as one compact mark.
- Hierarchy: the arch and substantial island are the principal mass. The lantern
  is the small warm focal point, clearly visible in front; trees stay secondary.
- Materials: matte painted wood and ceramic, warm limestone, restrained brass.
  This owner-requested material identity uses continuous credible 3D shading;
  animal anatomy, polygon fragmentation and rainbow-accessory rules do not apply.
- Camera: a gentle 70 mm product-photography perspective from roughly 35–40
  degrees above the miniature, showing its top, arch opening and physical base.
  The model chooses exact overlap and placement. Keep all objects complete and
  comfortably inset for a later rounded tile.
- Source colors: cream `#fff3d8`, stone `#eed7b1`, green `#668b70`, mint
  `#a8c7a9`, moss `#4f7455`, coral `#ce775b`, gold `#e7b552`, brass `#bd8940`.
  These are actual scene tokens; the generated Logo palette will be sampled
  separately from returned native pixels after approval.
- Future presentation: quiet sage/ivory paper, separate shallow contact shadow,
  a project-specific architectural model-making surface with pressed construction
  arcs, alignment ticks and a linked chapter route. Light/dark support textures
  use the separate Flare workflow; no animal habitat stencil is reused.
