## Direction

One exceptional die-cast F-22 stealth fighter desk model, caught in the quiet decisive instant of a shallow banking turn. Its broad diamond wings, twin canted tail fins and compact pointed fuselage should form a bold coherent mark. Use honest satin titanium, subtle blue-gray ceramic-coated panels, finely machined panel joins and a single warm amber smoked canopy. This is a collectible physical aircraft model, with convincing thickness and restrained material detail; no missiles firing, vapor trails, sky scene, pedestal, jet plume, labels or detached parts. A 70 mm product view from a three-quarter front/top angle, around 30 degrees elevation, should keep the entire wingspan, nose and both tail fins visible. Let the model select an expressive diagonal and an elegant slight bank. The substantial wings carry the icon, not an extremely thin side-on fuselage. Keep every tip complete within the white viewfinder.

## Separate presentation

Three swept flight lanes and short altitude ticks crossing a pale blue atmospheric field. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/dogfight` page remain separate deliverables.
