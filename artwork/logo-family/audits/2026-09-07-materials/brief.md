# Template material and architecture series

Dotty and Matrix form a coordinated pair of suspended thick square blocks with soft bevels, an elevated three-quarter camera around 45 degrees, and high-resolution physical materials. Basalt follows the owner’s subsequent correction: an obsidian-and-gemstone Forbidden City corner tower, viewed from a shallower angle around 25 degrees. The earlier plain basalt slab is preserved as rejected history and is never adopted.

- Dotty: opaque black-and-white checker ceramic on cool Cartesian drafting paper.
- Matrix: layered black/green mothership circuitry on pale mint routing paper, with a small internal arc and a separately rendered subtle lower spill.
- Basalt: obsidian architecture, emerald roof inlays, restrained ruby details and an amber finial on pale champagne architectural drawing paper.

Each setting has its own fine engineering geometry and a separate projected floor shadow. These explicitly requested materials and architecture replace the default animal drawing rules for this batch. Each returned image is native 2048 × 2048, accepted under the recorded named-batch waiver with an agent inspection of its exact hash. There are four model calls in total: one Dotty, one Matrix, and two Basalt generations.

All source whites are opaque before extraction. Physical surface highlights remain in the foreground; presentation fields, cast shadows and any outside light are independent. Dotty’s white checker cells are protected through measured, exterior-connected matting. Full-object placement leaves at least 128 native pixels of clearance from the actual rounded outline.

The source repositories, README variants, small app/browser marks and individual Hexly pages are updated together. Basalt is published from an origin-based isolated worktree because the owner’s main checkout has unrelated active development; its dirty-file hashes are preserved in source-before.json. Hexly and workflow publication similarly exclude unrelated local commits.
