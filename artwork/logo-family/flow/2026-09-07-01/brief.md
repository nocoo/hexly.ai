# Flow — Celadon mechanical keycap with a Chinese character

## Product

A Chinese pinyin input engine with language-model decoding and streaming prediction.

Source README at `9c225d805a09ab5fdbe4fa65486821057231fdcd`; historical catalogue descriptions are not the art brief.

## Direction

One elegant mechanical keyboard key, a substantial rounded celadon-green ceramic keycap with the single carefully formed Chinese character 中 inset in warm ivory on its top. A small portion of the dark switch housing and copper spring is visible beneath the cap, explaining its physical action without an exploded diagram. Capture the key rebounding very slightly after a press, the cap and mechanism still one close coherent object. The glazed ceramic should have rich restrained micro-reflections and subtly mottled celadon depth, with clean bevels and precise tiny metal details. No whole keyboard, hands, floating glyphs, decorative calligraphy or other words. A 70 mm product lens around 35 degrees elevation sees the character and two side faces. Let the model choose the charming tilt and physical proportions; the rounded key mass must remain legible at favicon size.

## Separate presentation

Staggered rounded key outlines with a small sequence of rising and falling tone strokes in celadon paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/flow` page remain separate deliverables.
