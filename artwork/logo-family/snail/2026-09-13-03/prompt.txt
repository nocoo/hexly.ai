Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: native square master for the new Snail animal identity, Hexly.ai brand family.

Create ONE original, clearly recognizable land SNAIL, complete within a naturally composed 2048 by 2048 square. This is the identity of a private video collection, organization and playback library. No words, letters, watermark, mockup frame, presentation tile or app UI.

REFERENCE ROLES, in submitted order:
1. Approved Frogie: connected irregular flat polygon drawing, lively but restrained animal character. Do not copy its frog anatomy, green color, musical notes or pose.
2. Approved Pew: bold iconic masses, economical dark and pale planes, visible shared facet edges. Do not copy its zebra, stripes, tongue or camera.
3. Approved Ocelot: sophisticated broad facets, compact expressive animal grouping and one close external accent. Do not copy its cat, spots or paper bird.
These are our own existing project identities used as style references. The previous Snail outline logo has been rejected and is deliberately NOT an input.

DRAWING AND PALETTE
Connected, irregular flat polygons and triangles across the shell AND soft body; broad readable planes first, smaller facets only to clarify the spiral and eye-stalks. Clear shared edges with no outlines. A terracotta shell dominates, anchored in Hexly #bf5c3c with related clay, rust and pale sandstone facets. The body uses Hexly ink #30372e and quiet warm olive-gray planes with pale mineral highlights; keep enough midtone planes for legibility on dark paper. Natural visible coiling shell structure, no round line-art logo, no eagle/bird or borrowed emblem. Sophisticated editorial geometry, crisp opaque colors, no smooth 3D gradients, glossy plastic, glass shards or scattered confetti.

ANATOMY AND THE ONE ACCENT
A real land snail: solid coiled shell carried on a continuous muscular foot, compact head, two naturally proportioned upper eye-stalks with small discreet dark tips, short lower feelers if they read cleanly. No human face, teeth, smile, huge toy eyes, arms or legs. The foot and shell form a substantial compact mark, not a thin scene. One small terracotta-red circular point is suspended close ahead of the curious snail's feelers, outside the main silhouette. It is the only external accent: a chosen moment to return to. Keep it near enough to belong to the mark and well within the frame. No extra trail dots, rainbow accessory or play-button graphic.

NATIVE FRAMING AND EXTRACTION
Find the composition in the generation itself. Preserve the entire shell, foot, tentacles and red point, with generous natural white breathing room around the group, including the future rounded app corners. About three quarters of the square should be occupied by the broad animal grouping; do not make a tiny animal in a big scene. No part touches or is clipped by the canvas. Do not zoom, crop or artificially flatten the anatomy to meet an edge. Solid opaque artwork on a perfectly uniform pure white matte (#ffffff). All anatomy including pale highlights stays distinct from white. Absolutely no cast shadow, ground plane, paper grain, background pattern, haze, bloom, vignette or translucent fringe in this isolated source. Background and very soft contact shadows will be separate authored layers later.

SPECIFIC CANDIDATE — SHELTERING
A close complete snail in a quiet near-PROFILE view, facing left with the head tucked slightly inward under the forward arc of its broad, rounded terracotta shell. The shell is an architectural spiral of generous polygon terraces, not concentric linework. The dark mineral body forms a low continuous sculptural base. A small red point hovers just in front of the extended feelers. A sheltered, thoughtful gesture, with clear living anatomy and no smile.
