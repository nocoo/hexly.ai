# signoff.now — Petrol enamel mechanical tally counter

## Product

Developer and Git repository activity analytics, collected locally and displayed in a web dashboard.

Source README at `92033c89d8074cda8ab848cb2faa20156da22d75`; historical catalogue descriptions are not the art brief.

## Direction

One premium hand-held mechanical tally counter, celebrating the act of making developer activity visible. A compact rounded petrol-blue enamel metal body, brushed brass thumb plunger and a small recessed mechanical number window with ivory digit drums. A short polished finger loop nestles close to the body so the whole assembly reads as one compact object. Capture the thumb plunger just beginning to return after a count, with one digit drum fractionally turning. The number wheels can show a simple short count, but no branding or large text. Make the physical mechanism tactile, believable and sophisticated: restrained enamel reflections, precision seams, softened metal edges. A 75 mm three-quarter product view at around 30 degrees elevation keeps the main counter mass dominant and the plunger clearly readable. This project measures developer/repository activity, not document signing; no pen, wax seal or signature.

## Separate presentation

Stepped tally bars, grouped count strokes and sparse ledger baselines on blue-gray paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/signoff-now` page remain separate deliverables.
