## Generation contract

- A recognizable rhinoceros head close-up; strong off-center composition,
  generous negative space, playful multicolor, connected irregular flat facets.
- The model chooses angle, gesture, palette and optional accents.
- No prescribed equipment, gym accessory, exact anatomy ratio or camera lens.
- Series: animal; source mode: isolated-white.
- Model: Azure `gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`, high quality, native 2048x2048 PNG.
- Uniform white matte; no text, background tile or cast shadows.
- Save raw bytes, sanitized request/response and pending hash-bound decision.
- Do not extract, composite, export or integrate until exact-byte approval.
