## Product

Fundly is a personal China mutual-fund, ETF, A-share and macro research tool.
Collection scripts and calculations run in Bun over a local SQLite database, and
a React interface compares funds, ranks them, screens ETFs and stocks, and reads
a macro board. The work it performs is patient, comparative and deep: many
instruments observed at once, compared on risk and return, without trading.

Primary interaction is a browser, so the series is
[hexly-animal](../../../../.agents/skills/hexly-animal/SKILL.md).

## Subject

An emperor penguin, head close-up.

The species choice is a product connection, not a generic mascot:
- Penguins dive deep and hold one line under pressure — the same research posture
  as drilling into fund history, drawdown and risk rather than price noise
- A colony dives as one group while each bird keeps its own path — hundreds of
  instruments compared, none traded
- The emperor's amber ear patch is already the family's warm accent, and Fundly's
  own interface token is `#e95d2b`

The species must read from the head alone: black hood, blunt ivory-edged beak,
pale cheek and the amber ear patch. No body, no props, no binoculars, no
instruments. The earlier identity's accessories are deliberately not carried
forward.

## Composition

Off-center, entering from the left, with the open field on the right. Per the
series contract: head at roughly 40–55% of the square, centroid at least 10% of
the edge length off center, one uninterrupted open region of at least a quarter
of the square, and no contact with the right edge.

## Palette

Lively mode, the series default: three to five clear hues carried by the bird's
own facets. Base reads as black and ivory; the amber ear patch is the living
accent. The model chooses the exact hues, their balance and the placement of any
additional facet color.

## Left to the model

Angle, gesture, head tilt, eye expression, facet boundaries and the precise hue
values. Composition, drawing language, mass, open field and the species-bearing
head are fixed by the series skill and this brief.
