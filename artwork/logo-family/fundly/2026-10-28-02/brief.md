# Fundly — emperor penguin, quiet palette, one small fish

## Request

Owner feedback on `2026-10-28-01`, 2026-10-28: the style is acceptable, but the
colour contrast is too bright and the character is not cute enough. Try again so
the colours echo the product's own theme colour, and give the animal one small
object as the only multicolour highlight. PEW is the reference for how that accent
works: a quiet animal with a single tiny colourful punctuation.

This is a fresh study. `2026-10-28-01` is rejected and stays archived.

## What changes from the rejected study

| Issue                       | Correction                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Colour contrast too bright  | Charcoal, deep slate, warm gray and ivory as tonal steps. No hard black-on-white jumps, no saturated orange field  |
| Not cute enough             | Bigger rounded head, wide-set large eye, short blunt beak, round cheek, baby proportions, calm friendly expression |
| No single multicolour point | One very small deep-sea fish held gently by the beak tip. It is the only saturated colour in the image             |
| Weak product tie            | The muted amber ear patch and the fish's coral-amber facets both echo Fundly's `#e95d2b`                           |

## Why a small fish

It is the penguin's natural prey, not an invented prop, so the species stays
coherent. A deep-sea fish also carries the product meaning: Fundly's work is
diving into fund history and surfacing the few things worth looking at. The fish
stays smaller than the head and attached at the mouth, never a floating second
subject.

## Subject

Emperor penguin, head close-up. Charcoal hood, ivory face and throat, muted amber
ear patch. No body, no feet, no scenery, no instruments.

## Composition

Off-center, entering from the left, open field on the right: head at roughly
40–55% of the square, centroid at least 10% of the edge length off center, one
uninterrupted open region of at least a quarter of the square, no contact with the
right edge.

## Palette

Quiet and low-contrast on the animal. Exactly one saturated multicolour area: the
small fish, whose facets move through coral, amber, teal and pale rose. Nothing
saturated on the hood, face or facets.

## Left to the model

Exact hues, facet boundaries, head tilt, the fish's species detail and the
placement of its colour facets.
