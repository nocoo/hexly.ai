A browser prairie shooter inspired by Journey of the Prairie King.

## Direction

A beautifully made miniature western arcade control box, one cohesive physical object. A compact warm walnut housing with rounded corners, a dark aged-brass control plate, one bold oxblood-red ball-top joystick and a small grouped pair of ochre and muted teal buttons. Subtle engraved prairie sun rays in the brass give the object its western character without words or decorative clutter. Capture the joystick just leaning into a move, as a decisive tactile instant; no human hand. The wood grain, softened brass edges, real fasteners and satin lacquer should feel lovingly crafted, with a readable joystick silhouette at small size. A 65 mm product perspective around 35 degrees above the object shows its top and one thick side. Let the image choose the exact rotation and proportions. No screen, full arcade cabinet, cables running out of frame, pixel art, floating bullets or extra props.

## Separate presentation

A low rising sun with short prairie grass cuts and concentric joystick-like arcs in warm sand paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/pew-game` page remain separate deliverables.
