## Direction

One finely crafted analog chess clock with two ivory circular faces set in a single compact curved walnut case. Small dark hands and subtle tick marks form readable opposing dials; use no numerals or lettering. Two short satin-brass plungers sit on top, one down and the other just raised, capturing the decisive instant when a discussion changes sides. A narrow oxblood edge detail supplies one restrained color accent. The visible grain, hand-finished edges, glass dial reflections and credible clock thickness should feel like a beautiful real instrument. A 75 mm view at roughly 20 degrees elevation and a modest three-quarter rotation preserves the paired-face symmetry without becoming perfectly frontal. No chess pieces, game board, stage or detached props.

## Separate presentation

Two offset dial arcs with alternating short speaking-turn marks on warm oak-toned paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/arena` page remain separate deliverables.
