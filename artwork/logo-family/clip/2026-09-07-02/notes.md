Both native responses and the withdrawn mantis finishing remain archived. This project used two Image 2 calls; study 02 is the selected local preview.

The owner waived the intermediate image handoff for this named batch. [The raw decision](raw-review.json) records delegated acceptance of the exact native bytes, with `ownerReviewedExactBytes: false`. The selected finishing is `01`. All frozen passes retain their own settings, tool snapshot, source decision, masks, independent background and shadows, ten export sizes and checksums.

## Framing and palette

The complete face, beak, hat and feather group have a documented conservative feature region with 225.5 px minimum clearance from the actual rounded outline and zero clipped feature pixels; see [the production measurement](preparation/framing-measurements.json). The original neck intentionally continues through the lower frame; its terminal border is not a clipped accessory. The 90% placement uses no synthetic continuation.

The [palette](palette.json) records exact native sRGB artwork samples separately from the designed **Windward feather relief** field and existing source UI tokens. The paper uses base `#baae91`, light `#e7dcc2`, shade `#8c7a5b` and motif `#65563b`. Transparent foregrounds contain no paper, grain or cast shadow.

The brim, beak and colorful feather silhouette carry the 32/16 px mark; individual eye facets and feather divisions soften at favicon size. Small app and browser marks use the transparent portrait, including its intentional lower neck entry.
