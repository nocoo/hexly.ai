# Pew study 01

Pew's existing faceted zebra is a liked identity. Preserve its three-quarter view toward the viewer's left, upright ears, near-eye wink, open far eye, striped ivory/charcoal face, dark muzzle, and small rainbow tongue. The owner specifically requested a natural, playful entrance from the frame instead of a circular neck cutoff.

Pew tracks token usage across AI coding tools and presents contribution graphs. Its live site is `https://pew.md`; its UI primary is `#851DED` with background `#F1F0F5`. Those are site tokens, not instructions to turn the zebra purple.

## Direction

- A large-animal head portrait, with a continuous neck entering from the lower-right part of the square. The face leans into view; the lower neck continues past the bottom canvas edge. No circular bust, floating neck end, vignette, or fade.
- Retain the liked anatomy, stripe rhythm, expression, and rainbow tongue. Broad, connected flat facets follow the approved Frogie drawing language. Avoid smooth 3D skin, realistic fur, and glass-like shards.
- Leave deliberate breathing space toward the upper left. Keep the ears, eyes, muzzle, and tongue away from the final 23% rounded corners; only the neck's anatomical continuation is intentionally cut by the frame.
- Proposed presentation: a soft pearl-gray/lilac field with very quiet curved motifs and restrained matte shadows. Final colors will be recorded after inspecting the generated art. Existing UI tokens stay separate.

## Reference order

1. Existing Pew logo: authoritative species, stripes, camera, wink, ears, dark muzzle, and rainbow tongue.
2. Approved Frogie finishing 02 icon: connected flat facets and the family's restrained matte presentation, not its anatomy, green palette, or music notes.
3. `ref01.jpeg`: animal-family board; tonal backgrounds and offset positions.
4. `ref02.jpeg`: close-ups; natural frame entry, calm negative space, and matte tactile quality.

## Generation and finishing

Request one native 2048 × 2048 high-quality PNG through Azure Foundry `gpt-image-2`, on uniform white. Preserve the raw result, exact prompt, sanitized request/response, reference hashes, and every finishing pass. Background design is a separate local layer.

Inspect the white/ivory stripe boundaries before extraction. Frogie's white threshold cannot be assumed safe for this animal. Preserve white anatomy and highlights, inspect both light and dark backdrops, and record intentional bottom-edge intersections instead of requiring an entirely transparent canvas border.

This is a candidate. The current Pew source project and production catalogue remain on their existing identity until a new candidate is selected. Pre-existing changes in Pew's `packages/web/next-env.d.ts` and `packages/web/tsconfig.json` are outside this study.
