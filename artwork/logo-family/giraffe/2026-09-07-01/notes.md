## Framing, color and exports

Protected features or the complete animal group have at least 164.64 px clearance from the actual 23% rounded outline. Deliberate neck/shoulder entries, where present, are recorded separately in framing evidence. [Framing evidence](verification/framing.json) records the actual outline measurement. [Matte evidence](verification/matte.json) verifies native opaque RGB. No stretched anatomy, cloned edge rows or hand-painted continuations are used.

Ten transparent, square and rounded PNG sizes cover 2048, 1024, 512, 256, 128, 64, 48, 32, 24 and 16 px. The transparent, pure-white, square and rounded masters share one placement. Ossicones, both ears, face and every leaf keep at least 164.64 px of rounded-edge clearance. A one-pixel placement correction preserves the native extraction and closes the lower neck entry. The historical original is 1408 px; the new artwork is native 2048 px.

The background is **Acacia canopy relief**: base `#79865b`, light `#b9c398`, shade `#4b5c39`, motif `#354629`. [palette.json](palette.json) stores exact native sRGB sample coordinates and separates artwork, presentation and existing product-theme evidence.
