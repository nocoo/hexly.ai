# ag-cli: brass pneumatic dispatch capsule

## Direction and authorization

## Product

`ag-cli` is a lightweight CLI runtime wrapper for the local Antigravity installation. External CLI bots (pi, Claude Code, Codex) dispatch tasks to Antigravity conversations over a structured JSON protocol, wait on events and collect results and artifacts. Source intake: `nocoo/ag-cli` at `ecbb2e79835f18ad3d7448d7be6224c6b74ecc4a` (local, 2 commits ahead of `origin/main`), `docs/01-overview-and-architecture.md`. It is a CLI tool with no website.

## Series and subject

`hexly-things`, `family.series: "material"`. One brass pneumatic-tube message capsule: a sealed carrier that is sent into a conduit and returns with a reply, the physical analogue of dispatching a task and collecting its result. A narrow enamel band and a stitched leather pull strap are crafted parts of the capsule, not separate props. No tube, station or scenery.

Materials: brushed brass shell, deep blue enamel band, waxed leather strap with contrast stitching, felt-lined rim.
