Create one original project identity for EAGLE: a golden eagle in the Hexly fragmented animal family. One native 2048 × 2048 PNG on a perfectly uniform pure white #FFFFFF background. No text.

REFERENCES: image 1 is the accepted Frogie identity; use its connected irregular flat polygons, clean shared edges, coherent large color planes, strong readable expression and compact visual mass. Do not copy the frog, green palette or notes. Images 2 and 3 inform only the quiet tactile presentation philosophy and decisive off-center framing; do not copy their smooth 3D rendering, backgrounds or accessories. The output contains only the eagle and its one accent on white. Background and soft paper shadows are a later independent pass.

SUBJECT: a golden eagle, a perceptive calm observer with dignified warmth and a slightly inquisitive turn. A broad head and short natural neck settle into substantial overlapping feathered shoulders and the roots of folded wings. Head-and-shoulder portrait, never a complete small eagle, and never an upright human chest. Powerful compact hooked yellow-ochre beak, clearly readable dark pupil with a tiny highlight, characteristic raptor brow, tawny-gold crown and warm dark umber plumage. Choose a natural three-quarter head turn, with the near eye fully clear and any far eye present only as anatomically appropriate. No cartoon oversized eyeballs, grin, angry sports mascot, crown or heraldic badge.

DECISIVE MOMENT: as if an 85mm portrait camera caught the eagle turning slightly to follow a breeze. Its beak gently holds one narrow, folded wind streamer which lifts into the adjacent negative space. Let the model choose the precise tilt and overlap. The compact head, short neck and broad folded wing roots carry the visual weight; shoulders continue naturally beyond the lower canvas edge. This is a square viewfinder crop of a living bird, not a bust cut into a circle, floating neck stump or oval medallion. Keep the entire crown, eye, beak, feather tips near the face and complete streamer safely inside the eventual rounded tile with generous breathing room, around 12 percent from the top and side edges. Only the broad natural shoulders may enter the bottom boundary.

ONE EXTERNAL INTEREST POINT: one short airborne wind streamer gently held at the beak tip, made of a few connected folded segments in restrained coral, amber, teal and blue. It forms one coherent curved gesture just beside the beak, small and subordinate to the eagle, visibly beyond the head silhouette. No additional confetti, stars, detached feathers, rainbow body markings or unrelated accessories. The expression and beak gesture connect the eagle to that streamer.

DRAWING: clearly visible connected flat color polygons and irregular triangles across the entire eagle, including its broad forehead, cheek, neck and shoulder plumage. Broad planes explain anatomy; smaller planes articulate eye, beak and feather overlaps. Crisp shared edges with no black outlines. Volume comes only from deliberate adjacent flat colors, not smooth shading, realistic feathers, photographic lighting, glossy plastic, beveled crystals, glass shards or uniformly tiny triangular noise. Distinct warm umber, chestnut, ochre and muted golden planes; keep saturated multiple colors exclusively in the single streamer.

EXTRACTION: white background is exactly uniform #FFFFFF, including unoccupied holes around the streamer. All anatomy and light feather planes remain fully opaque and visibly darker than white at the contour. No shadows outside the animal, no floor, gradient, halo, fog, glow, vignette, border, corner mask or enclosing shape. Crisp clean antialiased silhouette. Every shape belongs to the animal or its one streamer. Preserve natural bottom shoulder continuation; no fading. One finished image, not a presentation sheet or mockup.
