## Object and moment

One compact, finely crafted film clapperboard just before the clap. Its broad green enamel slate forms the main mass. The hinged upper clapstick is raised a little, leaving a clean visible wedge; charcoal and warm ivory diagonal inlays make both sticks immediately recognizable. Brushed nickel hinge plates, small fasteners, clean bevels and subtle enamel texture supply convincing physical detail. Sparse ivory slate rules suggest production fields without lettering, numbers or an added play symbol.

Use a gentle three-quarter product view, approximately 75 mm perspective and 20–25 degrees elevation. Keep the slate substantially front-facing and fully visible. The whole object must be compact, crisp, visually full, and comfortably inside the square. The lifting clapstick is the single secondary gesture; no additional props are needed.
