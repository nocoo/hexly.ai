Create one production-quality, exceptionally handsome scarlet macaw bird logo for RIO, in the established flat fragmented animal family. This is a new identity: there is no previous animal to preserve.

REFERENCE ROLES: Image 1 is the approved Frogie and defines connected irregular flat facets, clear silhouette and confident color-plane drawing. Do not copy the frog, its pose, green coloring or music notes. Images 2 and 3 define elegant off-center composition and quiet matte presentation; do NOT copy their smooth 3D animal rendering. The requested output is ONLY the isolated macaw and its one feather interest point on pure white. The colored background will be authored separately.

THE MOMENT: An assured, charismatic adult scarlet macaw has just turned its head to watch one small feather drifting beside its beak. Handsome, alert and cool, with a strong graphic eye and a powerful hooked macaw beak, no cartoon grin, human eyebrows, baby eyes, clothes or sunglasses. Preserve unmistakable macaw anatomy: sculpted broad forehead, ivory cheek patch, sweeping large hooked beak and short natural neck meeting substantial shoulders and folded wing roots. A three-quarter portrait faces toward viewer-right. One near eye is clearly visible; the far eye is naturally occluded by the head angle, not drawn in an impossible position.

COMPOSITION: Treat the square as an 85 mm portrait viewfinder catching a poised decisive moment. Build a bold, compact, visually full upper-body mass offset to the LEFT, balanced by a quiet pocket of space to the upper right. Head, chest and folded wing roots overlap naturally. The lower chest and wing continue beyond the BOTTOM edge as a natural portrait crop; no circular bust, floating stump, medallion, enclosing badge or disconnected neck. Leave comfortable margin above the head, around the beak, eye and complete feather for an eventual rounded tile. Let the exact tilt, camera distance and overlap follow the animal's gesture. No long thin neck, giant tail, full-body standing parrot or expansive wings that shrink the face.

ONE SECONDARY INTEREST POINT: A single small complete curved feather floats close to the beak in the upper-right negative space, watched by the macaw. Its faceted turquoise, golden-yellow and a little cobalt vane form ONE coherent elegant color group around a visible quill. It is roughly one fifth of the head's visual size, clearly subordinate, near enough to belong to the same mark. This is a recognizable feather, not loose polygon confetti. No additional flowers, notes, stars, sparkles, extra floating feathers or ornaments.

COLOR: A richly colorful natural scarlet macaw, with luminous scarlet/coral/crimson as the dominant head-and-chest family. Visible folded wing planes gather a controlled band of warm golden-yellow, a transition of emerald/turquoise and deep royal cobalt-blue. This multicolored plumage is an explicit owner request; keep a coherent avian color hierarchy rather than random rainbow patches. The cheek is opaque pale warm ivory, with limited charcoal facial markings, a sharp dark eye and a small clean highlight. A strong ivory-to-charcoal hooked beak anchors the expression. Keep the pale outer planes distinct from the white matte using subtle flat warm-gray/ivory planes, never a thick outline.

DRAWING: Connected irregular flat polygons and triangles with crisp shared edges across the main head, cheek, beak, chest AND wing surfaces. The whole bird must visibly speak the same faceted language as reference 1. Broad confident planes explain anatomy; use smaller planes only around the eye, beak and feather structure. Each facet has a uniform flat color; imply volume through the sequence of hue and value planes, not smooth airbrushed rendering. Preserve a readable face at 32 and 16 pixels. No tiny uniform triangle noise, black polygon outlines, fragmented holes, detached shards, glossy plastic, 3D figurine, crystal, realistic feathers or photographic textures.

SEPARATION AND DELIVERY: One native 2048 by 2048 PNG, high quality, on a perfectly uniform pure white #FFFFFF matte. All artwork is solid and opaque, including the ivory cheek and highlights. Crisp antialiased boundaries, no ambient gray halo, cast shadow, floor, bloom, haze, gradient background, tile, rounded mask, border, logo mockup, lettering, watermark or text. Only the intentional lower chest/wing portrait continuation meets the bottom canvas boundary; the complete expressive face, upper head, hooked beak and secondary feather remain safely inside. The bird is the clear primary mass and the feather its only companion.
