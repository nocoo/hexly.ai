## Art direction

- A handsome, assured scarlet macaw turns toward a single drifting feather. Mature natural macaw proportions, confident alert eye, powerful hooked beak, crisp ivory cheek and sweeping compact wing roots.
- Scarlet/coral main head and chest; restrained natural golden-yellow, emerald/teal and cobalt-blue wing grouping. The owner's multicolored plumage direction overrides the usual single-hue body default.
- Connected irregular flat facets across the head, cheek, beak, chest and wing. Broad planes dominate, small planes articulate the expression. No smooth figurine shading.
- An intentional left-offset upper-body portrait enters naturally through the bottom edge. Three-quarter turn toward viewer-right, with one clearly visible near eye and the far eye naturally occluded by the camera.
- One secondary interest point: a single small curved feather floating close to the beak in the upper-right negative space. Its turquoise and golden vanes make one coherent accent. The bird's gaze connects it to the feather; no detached confetti, extra notes or props.
- An 85 mm portrait analogy directs attention to the face and feather. The model chooses exact tilt and crop. Important features remain clear of eventual rounded corners; only the natural lower chest/wing continuation may cross the canvas.
- Separate presentation: quiet warm coral paper with unique broad tropical palm and heliconia leaf relief, inspired by the macaw's rainforest habitat. The foreground stays opaque on pure white for exterior-connected extraction. No baked-in tile, shadows or glow.
