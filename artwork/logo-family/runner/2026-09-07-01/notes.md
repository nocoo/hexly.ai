# Runner identity

Set the moment. Let it run.

## Source and acceptance

Azure Foundry gpt-image-2 returned one native 2048 × 2048 image for this project. The owner delegated intermediate acceptance for the named batch; raw-review.json records the exact hash and agent inspection, without claiming that the owner saw those bytes.

Selected finishing `01`. Every numbered finishing pass is frozen with the exact settings, tool snapshot, alpha, independent background, shadows, exports and checksums. The authorization and failed/superseded attempts remain in the archive.

## Framing and material

The whole object clears the actual 23% rounded outline by at least 213.5 px, with zero clipped foreground pixels. Every delivered representation uses the same uniform placement. No stretched anatomy, circular bust cut, discarded physical highlight or invented source resolution is used.

[palette.json](palette.json) records actual native sRGB colors and coordinates separately from the designed **Scheduled minutes** field and original product UI tokens. The field uses base `#c2ceb4`, light `#e0e8cf`, shade `#a8ba9b` and motif `#778d61`. Backgrounds, grain and cast shadows are separate from transparent app marks.

At 128/64 px, the emerald face and steel case remain clear. At 32/24/16 px, the compact silhouette and dominant colors carry recognition; fine material detail, printed marks and background lines naturally merge.
