# Runner — Emerald enamel mechanical stopwatch

## Product

A declarative macOS scheduler that runs AI jobs through launchd.

Source README at `3bd26f260c1dedf63faf4a616421b8592b975be0`; historical catalogue descriptions are not the art brief.

## Direction

One premium mechanical stopwatch, an emerald-green enamel dial inside a smoothly machined satin steel case, with a warm brass winding crown and a single short start button. A fine ivory timing hand has just begun to sweep, held sharply in one decisive instant, with no motion blur. Subtle ivory minute ticks and one small inset subdial communicate a real timing instrument without labels or excessive numerals. Give the enamel physical depth, the steel a soft brushed finish and the glass a controlled highlight that never hides the hand. A 75 mm close product perspective about 25 degrees above the face keeps the round body dominant, with the complete crown and button inside the frame. No chain, watch strap, calendar, running person, extra props or external glow.

## Separate presentation

An interrupted outer minute track, a shorter offset timing arc and grouped launch ticks on pistachio paper. The exact foreground, independent backdrop, shadows, ten export sizes, static comparison and `/logos/runner` page remain separate deliverables.
