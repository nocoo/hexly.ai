Decorative background surface for a Chinese-cultural colour atlas, drawn as a Dunhuang mural fragment covering the right side of the sheet. Neutral cream handmade paper in the page's own tone on the left two thirds; the right third carries a substantial painted motif: layered mineral pigment bands in azurite blue, malachite green and quiet cinnabar with gold-leaf flecks, flowing silk-ribbon curves and a weathered cracked plaster edge, sweeping from the upper right to the lower right and filling that whole side. Soft even daylight, matte pigment, restrained low chroma, museum-quiet. Orthographic flat material study, complete square canvas, no text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no user interface, no central emblem, no sparse speckles, no yellow cast.
