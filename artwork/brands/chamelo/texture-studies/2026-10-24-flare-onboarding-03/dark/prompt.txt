Decorative background surface for a Chinese-cultural colour atlas. Deep grotto teal-slate Dunhuang plaster with fine aggregate grain and a large quiet field, with one lifted plaster edge and a sparse gathering of mineral pigment flecks in azurite blue, malachite green and quiet cinnabar drifting into the lower-right area. Soft low light with detail visible in shadow, shallow believable relief. The detail stays in roughly a quarter of the sheet; the upper-left and centre remain plain plaster. Orthographic flat material study, complete square canvas, no text, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no user interface, no central emblem, no all-over pattern.
