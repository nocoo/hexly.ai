# Mico leaf texture brief

Mico is a local-first canvas where people and AI agents create together. Its existing logo is a candy-colored physical canvas; it remains unchanged. The owner explicitly requested a leaf texture for its Hexly archive, overriding the material-family default. The foliage is a decorative metaphor for ideas growing on a shared canvas, not an asserted habitat or product capability.

Use a loose arrangement of small rounded leaves and slender stems along the right and lower margins, with a quiet upper-left area for text. Match the Hexly paper and ink surfaces and neighboring botanical cards, not the candy colors of the independent Mico interface. Generate separate complete light and dark canvases with GPT Image Flare. Do not crop or claim seamless repetition.
