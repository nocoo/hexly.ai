# ag-cli: dispatch-conduit texture study

## Product → material → motif

ag-cli is a CLI runtime wrapper that lets external bots dispatch tasks to local Antigravity conversations and collect results. Source evidence: `nocoo/ag-cli` at `ecbb2e79835f18ad3d7448d7be6224c6b74ecc4a`, `docs/01-overview-and-architecture.md`, and the approved brass pneumatic capsule Logo (`artwork/logo-family/ag-cli/2026-11-14-01`, raw SHA-256 `5ecd369a…2cc8`).

The surface is a continuous matte paper working surface carrying pressed conduit traces: paired channel furrows with rounded elbows, small flange rings and bolt-seat dimples, and brushed micrograin. These are tactile traces, not a literal tube network, capsule, diagram or the Logo. Do not copy Linie's straight drafting guides, Dotty's ceramic cells, Matrix's circuit routing or Basalt's joinery.

## Collection construction

Same construction as the accepted Linie full-square specimens: shallow worked relief over the whole square, Hexly paper base (`#f0f0e9` / `#f8f8f2` light, `#1e2824` / `#27332c` dark), pale sage worked regions. The project's brass and blue stay in the Logo, never in the substrate.
