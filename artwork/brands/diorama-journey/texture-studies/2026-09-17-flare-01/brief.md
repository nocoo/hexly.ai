# Diorama Journey support textures

The original example carries one amber lantern through a gateway, lookout, workshop, bridge and garden. Model-making construction marks connect the physical miniature scenes with the shared chapter timeline. This is a working surface for a 3D storytelling tool, not a botanical habitat or a literal UI diagram.

Shallow arch construction arcs, paired alignment ticks, five small chapter stations connected by a gently turning route, and faint short measurement strokes, pressed into continuous model-making board.

Light and dark use the evidenced Hexly page tokens from src/styles/base.css. Flare generates two native 1024-square canvases. This named project is the complete generation scope. Raw owner review is required before delivery derivatives. Model choice follows the existing decorative-texture policy.
