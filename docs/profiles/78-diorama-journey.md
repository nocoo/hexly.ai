## Project goal

Turn source material into an original narrated miniature 3D journey. A recurring element connects the chapters; the same timeline produces a full film, chapter MP4 clips, subtitles and PPTX/PDF/ODP slides with speaker notes. Install the skill to create an independent project from its starter.

把原始材料转成原创的配音微缩 3D 旅程，以一个贯穿元素连接各个章节。同一条时间线导出完整影片、分章 MP4、字幕，以及带演讲备注的 PPTX/PDF/ODP 幻灯片。安装技能后，可从 starter 创建独立项目。

### A journey in one model

### Materials that tell the story

Cream architectural stone, matte sage surfaces, terracotta and brass give this storytelling skill a physical tool identity. Its continuous 3D volume and native material colors are preserved through transparent extraction.

奶油色建筑石材、哑光鼠尾草绿表面、陶土和黄铜，为叙事技能建立实体工具形象。透明提取保留连续的三维体积与原生材质颜色。

### Chapter gates on a working sheet

Pressed arch construction lines, paired registration ticks and a turning chapter route sit on sage model-making paper. The separate surface connects miniature scene construction with a continuous story timeline.

鼠尾草绿模型纸上压印拱门施工线、成对定位刻度与转折的章节路线，把微缩场景搭建和连续叙事时间线联系起来。
