## Project goal

Explore 246 Chinese learning cards with an adult, browse full-screen illustrations, hear browser speech and keep favorites on the device. Installable PWA; previously viewed content can work offline, but speech depends on the device.

由成人陪伴探索 246 张认知卡片，全屏翻看插画、收听浏览器语音并保存本机收藏。支持 PWA 安装与已缓存内容离线访问，语音能力依赖设备。
