## Project goal

Plan weekly strength and cardio, log real workouts and body measurements, and review personal progress in an Access-protected single-owner journal. Movement clips are illustrative previews, not professionally reviewed instruction.

在 Access 保护的个人日志中安排力量与有氧训练、记录真实训练与身体测量、回看进展。动作片段仅为演示预览，并非专业验收的教学。
