## Project goal

Talk naturally with your own Hermes agents from any browser. Rio brings GPT-Live voice conversations, real-time answers and local tool execution into one private workspace, with seamless switching between machines, gateways and agent profiles.

在任何浏览器中，与自己的 Hermes Agent 自然交谈。Rio 将 GPT-Live 实时语音、即时回答与本地工具执行融入私有工作区，随时切换机器、网关和 Agent 配置。

### A poised turn

A handsome scarlet macaw turns toward one nearby drifting feather. The left-offset head and broad folded wings form a compact portrait; the shoulder and chest continue naturally through the lower frame.

绯红金刚鹦鹉从容望向身旁飘落的一枚羽毛。偏左的头部与宽阔折翼构成饱满肖像，肩羽和胸部自然延伸至画面下方。

### Color across connected planes

Connected flat facets cover the crown, ivory cheek, hooked beak, chest and wings. Scarlet leads the natural gold, emerald, turquoise and cobalt plumage requested by the owner.

冠羽、象牙色面颊、钩喙、胸部和双翼均由连贯平面碎片构成。绯红为主，搭配金黄、翠绿、青绿与钴蓝，呈现明确要求的多彩羽色。

### Rainforest leaf relief

Palm fronds and broad heliconia leaves form a quiet warm coral paper field, inspired by the macaw’s tropical forest habitat. Authored geometry, fine grain and contact shadows stay separate from the animal.

棕榈叶与宽阔的赫蕉叶构成温暖、安静的珊瑚色纸面，呼应金刚鹦鹉的热带森林栖息地。独立绘制的纹样、细纸粒与接触阴影均与动物前景分离。
