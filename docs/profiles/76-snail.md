## Project goal

Build a private home for videos worth returning to: collect them, organize them into categories and tags, then browse and play them in one library. Snail is in development toward its first v0.1.0 release; the stack below is the source team’s confirmed implementation plan. This page publishes its Hexly brand identity independently of the product release.

为值得回看的视频建立私人资料库：收藏、分类、打标签，再集中浏览与播放。Snail 正在开发首个 v0.1.0 版本，下方是源团队确认的实现技术栈。本页先独立发布 Hexly 品牌档案，产品发布由 Snail 团队完成。

### One point of focus

The coil holds the collection; the terracotta point looks ahead. Keep both strokes and the point together. Use ink for small text and the real Hexly light/dark palettes.

回旋收拢收藏，陶土红点望向前方。保留完整线条与红点，正文用墨色，明暗版本沿用真实 Hexly 色板。

### Room around the mark

Keep one point diameter clear around the artwork: 24 units in a 256-unit canvas. Preserve its viewBox, proportions and outlined Space Grotesk wordmark.

图形四周至少留一枚红点直径：256 单位画布中的 24 单位。保留 viewBox、原始比例和转曲后的 Space Grotesk 字标。

### Small, still Snail

Mark 16px minimum; 24px preferred in navigation. Wordmark ≥72px wide; lockup ≥160px. Tabs use transparent SVG/ICO, app launchers use the square presentation.

标志最小 16px，导航推荐 24px；字标宽度至少 72px，组合至少 160px。页签用透明 SVG/ICO，应用启动图标用方形展示版。
