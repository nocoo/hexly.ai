## Project goal

Read thirty selected Chinese classics with your child, follow reading guidance, save local favorites and return offline. No accounts or remote personal storage.

与孩子阅读三十首精选古诗，查看亲子提示、保存本地收藏并离线重读，无账号或远程个人存储。

森林绿布面、层叠象牙白纸页与黄铜月牙保留连续物理明暗。

Fine staggered bookcloth weave and gently fanned paper-edge arcs, authored for the tangible poetry book rather than botanical animal habitat.

独立布纹、纸页弧线、浅接触阴影与细颗粒，不改变前景颜色。
