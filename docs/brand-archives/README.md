## Implementation batches and boundaries

1. Shared kit support and full-body generated animals: prove uncropped layouts, type bounds, exact original preservation, themed textures and real favicon exports. Extend to the 24 existing generated animal identities, retaining documented edge-entry portraits.
2. The 10 retained originals: keep bytes, margins, colors and historical source attribution. R2Shot (920px) and Hooky (900px) retain honest native-size limits.
3. The 19 material/architectural identities: preserve object and product meaning; use their approved camera/material language and distinct technical or tactile motifs.
4. The supplied Poké Pocket illustration: keep its 960px source, extraction record and third-party rights caveat. No generated-artwork claim or blanket MIT license for its character.

New immutable exports: `public/brands/<id>/v1.0.0/`. New source recipes: `artwork/brands/<id>/v1.0.0/`, with shared export tooling under `artwork/brands/collection-2026-09/`. Catalogue edits are confined to `brandKit` fields on the 54 selected JSON files. Shared component/model/schema/CSS, docs generators and focused tests are updated; no old asset path is deleted or overwritten.

Existing approved artwork is composed at full aspect ratio on explicitly authored Hero canvases; this is not a new GPT Image generation. Full-body art stays complete. A documented edge-entry portrait is kept against its intended baseline, without inventing missing anatomy. Square and wide canvases have separate layout recipes, not a crop of one another.

The family DNA is unchanged: actual Space Grotesk and Geist Mono, real paper/ink/terracotta tokens, generous spacing, rounded borders and a restrained red point outside the product artwork. Each new texture has project-specific geometry. A product’s existing colored foreground is never recolored to match Hexly.

Validation: source/manifest hashes, all downloads and native dimensions, unmasked transparent small marks and ICO entries, texture seams/contrast, legacy redirects, project navigation, desktop/mobile and both themes, real browser snapshots, build, required repository gates, then exact-SHA v0.10.0 publication and production HTTP verification.

## Color and identity scope — owner clarification

Hexly paper/ink/terracotta, typography, red-dot motif and editorial compositions apply **only** to this site's project introductions/brand archives and later Hexly-authored promotional material (video, Deck, social graphics). They do not prescribe any independent product site's palette, theme or UI. No product repository is written.

Every official project Logo keeps its original shape, proportions, core colors and exact file bytes. New theme surfaces may quote a small evidenced accent; neither a theme switch nor export recolors the Logo. `officialProjectIdentity` and `campaignInterpretation` are separate manifest roles. Any future GPT Image Hero is a Hexly campaign interpretation, not a replacement project Logo.

49 target family masters match their recorded project Logo byte for byte. **Basalt, clip, Fundly, DreamRO and Meowth** have different archived official/source identities and Hexly family studies; both remain accessible with independent checksums and the existing `review` status. The release does not promote those studies into the product repositories or imply their adoption.

The difference matrix's machine-readable `officialVersusCampaign` field records this per project. Tests compare the protected source-file hashes, decoded RGBA hashes and new master bytes, preserve all 75 original catalogue identity/theme records, and reject kits without the explicit `hexly-campaign` scope.
